How It Works

Four simple stages, from photo to color suggestions.

1

Upload or Capture

Choose a clear, well-lit face photo, or take one with your camera.

2

Detect the Face

The browser locates the face so only the right part of the image is analyzed.

3

Analyze Skin Color

Pixel colors from facial skin regions are sampled and averaged into one representative color.

4

Get Recommendations

See your approximate skin tone category and clothing colors that may complement it.

Why ChromaticFit?

Built to be simple, private, and honest about its limits.

Private by Design

Images are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Real Image Processing

Results come from analyzing the actual pixel colors of your image, not random guesses.

Simple Color Ideas

Get easy-to-follow clothing color suggestions and outfit combinations.

About This Project

ChromaticFit is a prototype made for a college Programming Logic and Design project, using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the Canvas API.
